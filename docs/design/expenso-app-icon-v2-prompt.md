## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: iOS app icon for Expenso, a simple personal expense tracker with income, spending categories and charts. Generate one polished finished icon, square 1024 x 1024. Background: full-bleed rich electric blue #2962FF with a very restrained darker blue gradient toward the bottom. Subject: a single large centered ivory-white geometric capital E monogram, designed out of three horizontal softly rounded bars joined by a vertical spine, with three staggered bar lengths subtly evoking a spending bar chart. Beautiful deliberate spacing, strong silhouette, instantly readable at tiny size. Style: premium minimal graphic design, nearly flat with subtly softened edges and a very slight dimensional highlight, crisp and calm. Mark occupies roughly 58 percent of the width, generous balanced negative space. Full square opaque background right to all four corners; do NOT draw a rounded-square outer tile, device mockup, border, or transparent corners. No words, currency symbols, additional objects, decorative sparkles, watermark or presentation sheet. Output just the icon artwork.
